Single Name Final Pay Vouchers created during World War I and World War II are an alternate source of service information in the event a file was lost in the 1973 fire. The following examples assist to interpret the information that can be found in these records. Information located within easily identifiable fields in these records includes: The veteran's serial (service) number (if available) is typically found in the following location on the pay voucher: Name Rank Unit assignment at discharge Date and place of enlistment Place and date of discharge Character of service Discharge authority
